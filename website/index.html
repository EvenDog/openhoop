Every tool and setting sits in plain view, and resting the pointer on any of them explains what it does.

T

Every tool in reach.

Each tool has its own button, with its name and shortcut a hover away, so nothing hides in a menu.

Type a position, size or angle in the precision bar, and watch stitch counts and sewing time update as you work.

A work in progress

Early days. Plenty to make.

OpenHoop is in active development. Its designs have been sewn on a Brother SE600, and stitch settings keep improving from version to version.

Version 0.7.3 is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. You’ll need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 or later. Test-sew a design before stitching on anything precious.
